Success Strategies

Online Exam Success Guide

Online Exam Strategy: Test your technology in advance, ensure a quiet environment, eliminate distractions, and have your materials organized before starting.

Online Test-Taking Strategies:

  • Technical Check: Test your internet connection and browser compatibility 30 minutes before exam time
  • Time Management: Monitor the timer, allocate time based on question points, flag difficult questions
  • Read Carefully: Look for keywords like "first," "best," "contraindicated," "priority" in online format
  • Save Frequently: If allowed, save your progress periodically to avoid losing work
Memory Aid for Medical Specialties:
  • Cardiology: Heart sounds, EKGs, hypertension
  • Pulmonology: Spirometry, asthma, COPD care
  • Gastroenterology: Digestive procedures, nutrition
  • Endocrinology: Diabetes management, hormone disorders
