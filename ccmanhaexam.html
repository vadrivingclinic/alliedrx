CCMA Exam Preparation

CCMA Exam Success Guide

Preparing for your NHA CCMA certification exam requires mastery of both clinical skills and administrative knowledge specific to medical assisting.

Key Clinical Skills to Review:

  • Patient Assessment: Vital signs, medical history, patient positioning
  • Clinical Procedures: Injections, venipuncture, EKG, spirometry
  • Specimen Collection: Blood, urine, throat cultures, wound cultures
  • Patient Education: Medication instructions, treatment plans, preventive care
  • Infection Control: Sterile technique, PPE, OSHA guidelines
Administrative Knowledge Areas:
  • Medical terminology and abbreviations
  • Electronic health records (EHR) documentation
  • Appointment scheduling and patient flow
  • Medical coding basics (CPT, ICD-10)
  • Insurance processing and billing procedures
  • Legal and ethical guidelines (HIPAA, consent)

Registration Checklist:

  • Create account at nhanow.com
  • Select "Clinical Medical Assistant" certification
  • Choose testing method (in-person or online proctored)
  • Select exam date, time, and location
  • Complete payment and save confirmation

Understanding Your CCMA Results

Your NHA score report provides detailed performance analysis:

  • Pass/Fail Status: Certification achievement
  • Performance by Domain: Clinical vs. Administrative areas
  • Detailed Breakdown: Specific skill areas within each domain
  • Certification Details: Validity period and renewal requirements
  • Retake Information: If needed, scheduling details
After Passing Your CCMA Exam:
  • Digital badge available within 2-3 business days
  • Physical certificate mailed within 2-3 weeks
  • Update resume with "CCMA" credential
  • Share certification with potential employers
  • Plan for continuing education (10 CE credits every 2 years)
