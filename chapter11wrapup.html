Step 2

Key Vocabulary Flashcards

Select each card to reveal the definition. Then try to explain each term in your own words—as if you were teaching a new team member in the office.

Glycosuria
The abnormal presence of glucose in the urine, often indicating poorly controlled diabetes.
Gluconeogenesis
The formation of glucose in the liver from proteins and fats, which can contribute to hyperglycemia.
Diabetic Retinopathy
A complication of diabetes involving microaneurysms and weakness in retinal capillaries, leading to vision problems.
Polydipsia
Excessive thirst, one of the classic symptoms of hyperglycemia and diabetes.
Nocturia
Excessive urination during the night, often disrupting sleep in patients with diabetes.
Growth Hormone (GH)
Also called somatotropic hormone; stimulates tissue growth and regulates glucose metabolism.
