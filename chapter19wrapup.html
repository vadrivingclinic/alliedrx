Clinical Knowledge

Common Fracture Types

Review these common types of fractures seen in orthopedic practice:

Greenstick Fracture

Incomplete fracture where bone bends and cracks but doesn't break completely. Common in children.

Example: Pediatric forearm fractures

Comminuted Fracture

Bone breaks into three or more pieces. Often requires surgical intervention.

Example: High-impact trauma fractures

Spiral Fracture

Bone breaks in a helical pattern along its length. Often caused by twisting forces.

Example: Sports injuries from twisting

Compound (Open) Fracture

Bone breaks through the skin. High risk of infection requires immediate attention.

Example: Motor vehicle accident injuries

Kaiwan's Sports Medicine Challenge

Kaiwan's Scenario

Kaiwan works at Sports Medicine Associates, assisting orthopedic surgeons including Dr. Alexander, the team physician for a professional baseball team. He must prepare for various orthopedic procedures.

Common Orthopedic Procedures:

Cast Application & Removal
Medical assistant prepares materials, assists with application, provides patient education, and assists with cast removal using oscillating saw.
Cold Therapy Application
Apply cold packs to reduce inflammation, swelling, and pain in acute injuries (20 minutes on, 20 minutes off).
Hot Moist Heat Application
Used for chronic conditions to increase blood flow, reduce stiffness (15-20 minute applications).
Crutch Fitting & Training
Properly fit crutches (2-3 finger widths between armpit and pad) and teach correct gait patterns.

Key Terminology

Orthopedic Vocabulary

Master these essential orthopedic terms for effective communication and documentation:

Arthritis
Inflammation of a joint causing pain, swelling, and stiffness. Common types include osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis.
Crepitation
A dry, crackling sound or sensation caused by bone fragments rubbing together or air in subcutaneous tissue.
Inflammation
Tissue reaction to trauma or disease characterized by redness, heat, swelling, and pain (cardinal signs).
Corticosteroids
Anti-inflammatory hormones, natural or synthetic, used to reduce inflammation in orthopedic conditions.
