Step 2

Key Vocabulary Flashcards

Select each card to reveal the definition. Then try to explain each term in your own words—as if you were teaching a new team member in Dr. Samuelson's practice.

Cilia
Hairlike projections capable of movement; in the lungs, cilia wave to move unwanted substances (mucus, dust, pus) upward. Destroyed by smoking, leading to impaired clearance.
Chronic Bronchitis
Recurrent inflammation of the membranes lining the bronchial tubes, characterized by chronic cough with sputum production for at least 3 months in 2 consecutive years.
Hypercapnia
Excess levels of carbon dioxide in the blood, often resulting from hypoventilation or respiratory failure in conditions like COPD.
Bronchiectasis
Dilation of the bronchi and bronchioles associated with secondary infection or ciliary dysfunction, leading to chronic cough with large amounts of purulent sputum.
Cell-Mediated Immunity
An immune response that occurs from the action of T lymphocytes rather than from antibody production. Important in defense against intracellular pathogens like tuberculosis.
Metastatic
Pertaining to the process by which cancerous cells spread from the site of origin to a distant site via lymph and blood circulation, common in advanced lung cancer.
