When explaining procedures to patients:
• "Ablation" → "We'll remove the problematic tissue"
• "Ischemia" → "Your heart muscle isn't getting enough oxygen"
• "Occlude" → "The artery is blocked"

Completed Paragraph:
Blood enters the right atrium from the body via the superior and inferior vena cava. It passes through the tricuspid valve into the right ventricle. The right ventricle pumps blood through the pulmonary valve into the pulmonary artery to the lungs for oxygenation. Oxygenated blood returns to the left atrium, passes through the mitral valve into the left ventricle, and is pumped through the aortic valve to the rest of the body.

Risk Factor Categorization

Sort the risk factors into modifiable (can be changed) and non-modifiable (cannot be changed).

📅 Non-Modifiable Risk Factors

✓ Feedback
Correct Sorting:
Modifiable: Smoking, High cholesterol, Physical inactivity, Uncontrolled diabetes, Obesity
Non-Modifiable: Age, Family history, Male gender
Patient Education Focus:
• Emphasize modifiable factors during counseling
• For non-modifiable factors, focus on increased monitoring
• "You can't change your family history, but you can control your blood pressure!"

Metabolic Syndrome: A cluster of modifiable factors (obesity, high BP, high blood sugar, abnormal cholesterol) that dramatically increases heart disease risk.
