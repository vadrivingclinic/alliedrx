Key Vocabulary Flashcards

Select each card to reveal the definition. Then try to explain each term in your own words—as if you were teaching a new team member in Dr. Lee's cardiology practice.

Sinoatrial (SA) Node
The natural pacemaker of the heart, located in the right atrium. It initiates the electrical impulse that begins each cardiac cycle.
Atrioventricular (AV) Node
The part of the cardiac conduction system between the atria and ventricles that delays the electrical impulse, allowing atrial contraction to complete before ventricular contraction begins.
Bundle of His
Specialized muscle fibers that conduct electrical impulses from the AV node to the ventricular myocardium via the right and left bundle branches.
Leads
Electrical connections attached to the body to record heart activity; in a 12-lead ECG, these provide different electrical views of the heart from various anatomical perspectives.
Ischemia
Decreased blood flow to a body part or organ, caused by constriction or blockage of the supplying artery. On ECG, often shows as ST segment depression or T wave inversion.
Cardioversion
The use of electroshock to convert an abnormal cardiac rhythm to a normal one. Can be synchronized (timed with R wave) or unsynchronized (defibrillation).
