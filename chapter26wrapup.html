Step 2

Key Vocabulary Flashcards

Select each card to reveal the definition. Then try to explain each term in your own words—as if you were teaching a new team member at Metro Urgicenter.

Air Kerma (Gyₐ)
The SI unit for radiation exposure that represents the amount of radiation in the air reaching the patient. Measured in Gray, with subscript "a" indicating it measures radiation in air.
Anteroposterior (AP) Projection
A frontal projection where the central x-ray beam enters the front of the patient and exits the back to reach the image receptor. The patient faces the x-ray tube.
Angiography
The process of producing an image of blood vessels using an iodine contrast medium to visualize vascular structures and identify abnormalities.
Angioplasty
An interventional technique where a catheter is used to open or widen a blood vessel to improve circulation, often performed under fluoroscopic guidance.
Anterior
The front part of the body or body part while in anatomic position. Used to describe radiographic positioning and anatomical relationships.
Angiocardiography
Radiography of both the heart and great vessels using an iodinated contrast medium to assess cardiac chambers, valves, and major blood vessels.
