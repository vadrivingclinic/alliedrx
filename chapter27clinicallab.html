🩺 Clinical Laboratory Insights

  • Regulatory Compliance: CLIA categorizes tests by complexity: Waived (simple, low risk), Moderate (requires training), High (requires specialized expertise). Always verify test categorization.
  • Safety First: OSHA requires accessible Safety Data Sheets (SDS) for all hazardous chemicals. Eye wash stations must be tested weekly and accessible within 10 seconds.
  • Quality Control: Document daily QC procedures. Out-of-range QC requires corrective action before patient testing. Maintain temperature logs for all laboratory equipment.
  • Specimen Integrity: Follow chain of custody for legal specimens. Document collection time, handling, and storage conditions. Improper handling invalidates test results.
  • Documentation: Laboratory requisitions must include: Patient ID, test requested, collection date/time, ordering provider, and clinical information. Missing information delays testing.

📋 Documentation & Compliance

  • CLIA Documentation: Maintain CLIA certificate, personnel competency records, QC documentation, and proficiency testing results for 2 years minimum.
  • OSHA Requirements: Document safety training annually, maintain SDS access, record exposure incidents, and conduct regular safety inspections.
  • Patient Results: Report critical values to provider immediately and document notification time, method, and person notified. Follow facility policies for normal/abnormal results.
  • Equipment Maintenance: Log calibration dates, maintenance, and repairs. Document out-of-service equipment and alternative testing arrangements.
