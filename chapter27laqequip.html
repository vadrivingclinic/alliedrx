✓ Equipment Mastery Complete!
Key Equipment Functions:
1. Centrifuge: Separates substances by density (RBCs from plasma)
2. Incubator: Maintains 35-37°C for microbial cultures
3. Autoclave: Sterilizes at 121°C, 15 psi for 15-30 minutes

Equipment Safety:
• Always balance centrifuge tubes before operation
• Keep incubator doors closed to maintain temperature
• Allow autoclave to cool before opening
• Document all maintenance and calibration dates

Measurement Standards: 1 mL = 1 cc, 1000 mg = 1 g, 2:45 PM = 1445 military time. Always document units with measurements.

Professional Standards:
1. Chain of Custody: Document every handler, use tamper-evident seals
2. Critical Values: Report immediately, document notification
3. Ethical Practice: Never falsify documentation, maintain integrity
4. Patient Education: Use teach-back method, assess understanding

Legal Responsibilities:
• Document errors with date, time, and corrective action
• Report abnormal results within time limits specified by policy
• Maintain confidentiality of all patient information
• Follow facility protocols for incident reporting

Quality Standards: All documentation must be accurate, timely, and complete. Use only approved abbreviations. Never use correction fluid - draw a single line, initial, and date.

🩺 Laboratory Excellence Standards

  • Equipment Competency: Daily centrifuge maintenance includes balancing tubes, checking speed calibration, and cleaning spills immediately. Incubators must maintain 35-37°C for accurate culture growth.
  • Measurement Accuracy: Report temperatures in both °F and °C for international compatibility. 1 mL = 1 cc exactly. Military time prevents AM/PM errors (2:45 PM = 1445).
  • Chain of Custody: Legal specimens require witnessed collection, tamper-evident seals, and documentation of every transfer. Breaks in chain invalidate results in court.
  • Critical Value Protocol: Report within 15 minutes. Document time, method (phone/verbal), person notified, and response. Common critical values: glucose <50 or >400, K+ <2.5 or >6.5, Na+ <120 or >160.
  • Ethical Practice: Never backdate, alter, or falsify records. Document errors honestly. Maintain professional boundaries with patients and colleagues.

📋 Documentation & Legal Standards

  • CLIA Documentation: Maintain records for 2 years minimum. Include personnel competency, QC results, proficiency testing, and instrument maintenance. Waived tests still require training documentation.
  • OSHA Compliance: Annual bloodborne pathogen training records, accessible SDS, exposure incident reports. Document sharps injuries within 24 hours.
  • Patient Education Documentation: Record teaching methods used, materials provided, and patient understanding assessment. Use teach-back method: "Please show me how you'll do this at home."
  • Result Reporting: Document normal and abnormal result communication. Use only approved abbreviations. No pencil entries - permanent ink only with proper error correction.
