Step 2

Key Vocabulary Flashcards

Select each card to reveal the definition. Then try to explain each term in your own words—as if you were teaching a new team member about laboratory procedures.

Aliquot
A portion of a well-mixed sample removed for testing. Ensures representative sampling and allows multiple tests from one specimen while maintaining specimen integrity.
Analyte
The substance or chemical being analyzed or detected in a specimen. Examples include glucose in blood, protein in urine, or specific antibodies in serum.
Calibration
Determining the accuracy of an instrument by comparing its output with that of a known standard or another instrument known to be accurate. Essential for reliable test results.
Anticoagulants
Chemicals added to a blood sample after collection to prevent clotting. Examples include EDTA, heparin, and citrate, each used for specific laboratory tests.
Caustic
Capable of burning, corroding, or damaging tissue by chemical action. Many laboratory reagents are caustic and require proper handling and PPE (personal protective equipment).
Chain of Custody
A legal process that documents the handling of a specimen from collection through testing to final disposition. Critical for forensic and legal cases to ensure specimen integrity.
