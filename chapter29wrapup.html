Step 2

Key Vocabulary Flashcards

Select each card to reveal the definition. Then try to explain each term in your own words—as if you were teaching a new team member in the clinic.

Venipuncture
The process of collecting blood from a vein using a needle, typically performed with an evacuated tube system, syringe, or winged infusion set.
Phlebotomy
The practice of drawing blood from patients for clinical testing, transfusions, donations, or research.
Hemolysis
The rupture or destruction of red blood cells, which can occur during blood collection and invalidate test results.
Order of Draw
The specified sequence in which blood collection tubes should be filled to prevent cross-contamination of additives between tubes.
Capillary Puncture
A method of obtaining blood by puncturing the skin, typically on the fingertip or heel, used for small volume tests or when venipuncture is not feasible.
Safety-Engineered Device
A needle or sharp device with built-in safety features to prevent accidental needlesticks after use, as required by OSHA regulations.
