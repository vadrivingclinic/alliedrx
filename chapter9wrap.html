Essential Terms

Pharmacology Vocabulary

Select each card to reveal definitions. Master these terms for safe medication administration.

Therapeutic Range
The blood concentration of a drug that produces the desired effect without causing toxicity. Maintaining levels within this range is crucial for safe, effective treatment.
Enteric Coated
An oral medication coated to resist stomach acid, ensuring absorption in the small intestine. These should NOT be crushed or chewed.
Pharmacology
The science of drugs, including their origin, nature, chemistry, effects, and uses. Clinical pharmacology studies drug effects in medical treatment.
Pharmacokinetics
What the body does to a drug: absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion (ADME). Understanding this helps predict drug behavior.

Safety Focus

Prescription Accuracy & Safety

As Kathy works with e-prescriptions, she must ensure accuracy and patient safety. Consider these critical elements:

  • Rights of Medication Administration: Right patient, drug, dose, route, time, documentation
  • E-prescription Requirements: Identity proofing, two-factor authentication, audit trails
  • Patient Education: Purpose, side effects, administration instructions, storage
  • Age Considerations: Pediatric dosing, geriatric metabolism changes, polypharmacy risks
