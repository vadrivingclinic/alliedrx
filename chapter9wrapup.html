Key Terminology

Pharmacology Math Vocabulary

Master these essential terms for accurate medication calculations:

Unit Dose
Method used by pharmacy to prepare individual doses of medication; a dose prepared in an individual packet for convenience and safety (e.g., blister packs).
Dispense
To prepare a drug for administration; includes calculating, measuring, and preparing the medication for patient use.
Reconstitution
Process of adding liquid to a powdered medication to prepare it for administration; requires precise measurement and mixing.
Therapeutic Range
Blood concentration of a drug that produces desired effects without toxicity; accurate dosing is critical to maintain levels within this range.
