🧠 Key Anatomical Insights

  • Heart Location: The heart sits in the mediastinum between the lungs. The apex points inferiorly and leftward - this is where you auscultate the apical pulse.
  • Cardiac Layers: Epicardium (outer), Myocardium (muscle), Endocardium (inner lining). The myocardium is thickest in the left ventricle.
  • Blood Flow Pathway: Body → SVC/IVC → RA → Tricuspid → RV → Pulmonary valve → Pulmonary arteries → Lungs → Pulmonary veins → LA → Mitral → LV → Aortic valve → Aorta → Body
  • Coronary Supply: LAD = anterior wall/septum, Circumflex = lateral wall, RCA = inferior wall/conduction system. Blockages in these arteries cause specific ECG changes.
  • Clinical Correlation: Understanding this anatomy is essential for interpreting ECGs. Different heart regions correspond to specific ECG leads.

📚 Review These Key Terms

  • Mediastinum: Central thoracic compartment containing heart, great vessels
  • Myocardium: Cardiac muscle layer responsible for contraction
  • LAD (Left Anterior Descending): "Widowmaker" artery supplying anterior wall
  • Pulmonary Circulation: Right heart → Lungs → Left heart
  • Systemic Circulation: Left heart → Body → Right heart
