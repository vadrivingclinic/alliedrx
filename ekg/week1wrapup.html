Essential Terms

Cardiac Vocabulary

Select each card to reveal definitions. Master these terms for understanding pathology.

Ischemia
Decreased supply of oxygenated blood to a body part or organ. In the heart, this is reversible if treated quickly.
Infarction
Death of tissue because of an inadequate blood supply. Unlike ischemia, this damage is irreversible.
Stroke Volume
The amount of blood ejected from a ventricle with each heartbeat. It determines cardiac output along with heart rate.
Mediastinum
The middle area of the thoracic cavity containing the heart, great vessels, trachea, and esophagus.

Clinical Application

Acute Coronary Syndromes

"Time is Muscle." You've learned about ACS (Unstable Angina, NSTEMI, STEMI).

  • Ischemia: Lack of oxygen. Reversible. Often shows ST depression.
  • Injury: Acute damage. Often shows ST Elevation (STEMI).
  • Infarction: Tissue death. Irreversible. Pathologic Q waves.
