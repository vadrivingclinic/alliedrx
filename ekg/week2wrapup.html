Essential Terms

Electrophysiology Vocabulary

Select each card to reveal definitions.

Depolarization
The electrical event where the inside of the cardiac cell becomes positively charged, leading to contraction.
Repolarization
The return of cardiac cells to their resting negative state, corresponding to relaxation.
Artifact
Interference or "static" on the ECG tracing caused by movement, loose electrodes, or electrical interference.
Isoelectric Line
The flat baseline of the ECG that represents a period of no electrical activity (rest).

Clinical Application

Reducing Artifact

Clean Tracings Matter. "Garbage in, garbage out." If the electrodes aren't placed correctly, the machine cannot read the heart's signals accurately.

  • Skin Prep: Clean with alcohol, dry thoroughly, and shave hair if necessary for better contact.
  • Patient Comfort: Shivering causes somatic tremor artifact. Keep the patient warm and relaxed.
  • Wire Management: Ensure wires aren't pulling on electrodes to prevent wandering baseline.
