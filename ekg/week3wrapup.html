Essential Terms

Sinus Vocabulary

Select each card to reveal definitions.

Sinus Arrhythmia
A rhythm originating in the SA node that is irregular; often varies with respiratory cycle (faster with inspiration).
Sinus Arrest
A pause in the rhythm caused by the failure of the SA node to initiate an impulse. The rhythm does NOT resume on time.
SA Block
An impulse is generated by the SA node but blocked from exiting. The rhythm resumes exactly when the next beat was due.
Sinus Tachycardia
A regular rhythm from the SA node with a rate between 101 and 180 beats per minute.

Clinical Application

Treat the Patient

"Treat the patient, not the monitor."

  • Symptoms: Dizziness, chest pain, shortness of breath, hypotension.
  • Stability: Is the patient perfusing? (Warm skin, normal mental status).
