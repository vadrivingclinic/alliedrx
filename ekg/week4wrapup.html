Essential Terms

Atrial Vocabulary

Select each card to reveal definitions.

Vagal Maneuver
Physical actions (bearing down, coughing) that stimulate the vagus nerve to slow the heart rate. First line for stable SVT.
Cardioversion
Synchronized electrical shock used to convert unstable rhythms (like A-Fib/Flutter/SVT) back to sinus rhythm.
Aberrant Conduction
When a supraventricular impulse travels through the ventricles abnormally, resulting in a wide QRS complex.
Paroxysmal
A rhythm that starts and stops suddenly (e.g., Paroxysmal SVT).

Clinical Application

Hemodynamics

"Atrial Kick" contributes 20-30% of cardiac output. When the atria flutter or fibrillate, this kick is lost.

  • Rapid Ventricular Response: If the ventricles beat too fast (>100 bpm), they don't have time to fill, reducing cardiac output further.
  • Controlled Response: Keeping the rate < 100 bpm helps maintain blood pressure even without the atrial kick.
