Essential Terms

Ventricular Vocabulary

Select each card to reveal definitions.

Torsades de Pointes
"Twisting of the Points." A specific type of Polymorphic VT often caused by low magnesium or prolonged QT interval.
Compensatory Pause
A pause following a PVC that allows the heart to reset. The next beat occurs exactly when it would have if the PVC hadn't happened.
Agonal Rhythm
A dying heart rhythm with wide, irregular beats at a rate less than 20 bpm. Usually pulseless.
Interpolated PVC
A PVC that squeezes in between two normal beats without disturbing the underlying rhythm or causing a pause.

Clinical Application

The Pulse Check

"Treat the patient, not the monitor." Nowhere is this more true than with Ventricular Tachycardia.

  • Stable VT: Patient has a pulse, normal BP, is awake. Treatment: Meds (Amiodarone) or Cardioversion.
  • Pulseless VT: Patient is dead. Treatment: Immediate Defibrillation and CPR.
