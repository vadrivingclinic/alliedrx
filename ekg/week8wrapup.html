Essential Terms

Pacemaker Vocabulary

Select each card to reveal definitions.

Capture
Depolarization of the heart muscle after an electrical stimulus (spike). Seen as a P-wave or QRS following the spike.
Sensitivity
The ability of the pacemaker to "see" or sense the patient's intrinsic electrical activity.
Inhibition
The pacemaker's response to sensing an intrinsic beat: it holds (inhibits) its own fire to prevent competition.
Threshold
The minimum amount of electrical energy (milliamperes) required to consistently capture the heart.

Clinical Application

Mechanical Capture

"Electrical capture does not guarantee mechanical capture."

  • The Monitor: Shows electrical spikes and QRS complexes.
  • The Patient: May have Pulseless Electrical Activity (PEA) even with a "perfect" paced rhythm on screen.
