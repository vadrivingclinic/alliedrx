Pharmacy Technician Program Materials

Below are the required textbooks and supplies for the Pharmacy Technician program. These materials are essential for classroom instruction, hands-on labs, and clinical training.

Textbook

Mosby's Pharmacy Technician: Principles and Practice, 5th Edition

Required

This comprehensive 5th edition textbook provides essential knowledge for pharmacy technicians, covering key principles and practical applications in pharmacy practice. Includes practice questions, case studies, and updated regulations.

Program Requirements

Important Information

  • ✓ Textbook: Mosby's Pharmacy Technician: Principles and Practice, 5th Edition is required for all coursework and examinations.
  • ✓ Scrubs: Professional medical scrubs must be worn during all hands-on laboratory sessions and clinical rotations. Multiple pockets and comfortable, durable fabric are recommended.
  • ✓ Vendor Flexibility: Materials can be purchased from any vendor of your choice (Amazon, local bookstores, uniform retailers, etc.). The provided links are examples only.
  • ✓ Timeline: All required materials should be acquired before classes begin. Allow sufficient time for ordering and delivery.
