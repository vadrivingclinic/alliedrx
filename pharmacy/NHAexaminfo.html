How to Apply for Your NHA Exam

Step 1: Log into Your Account
Log into your Candidate account at NHANOW.com.
Make sure your account is linked with your current Institution.
Step 2: Select "Apply"
Select Apply on the left side of your page under Applications.
Step 3: Choose Your Exam
Under Choose an exam select the certification type and verify that the correct Institution is shown next to Current Institution.
If the wrong institution is listed, click "Update Institution" to edit your account.
Step 4: Read & Agree to Attestation
Read the Attestation and if applicable, agree to the terms at the bottom of the page.
If you cannot agree to the terms, you may not be eligible to sit for the exam.
Step 5: Choose Exam Section
Click the exam date from the list of available dates and then click Continue.
Step 6: Answer Prerequisite Questions
Answer all prerequisite questions, then choose Register for Another Exam or Checkout.
Step 7: Verify Information
On the Checkout page, verify your Basic/Billing information and then click the Continue Checkout tab at the bottom of the page.
Step 8: Complete Payment
Provide payment type as needed and click Purchase Items.
A confirmation email will be sent to the email address associated with this account.

✅ Passing Your NHA Exam

Upon successful completion of your NHA certification exam, you'll become nationally certified in your healthcare specialty. This certification is recognized by employers across the United States and demonstrates your competency and commitment to professional standards in healthcare.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know which NHA certification is right for me? +
Consider your career goals, interests, and the healthcare setting you want to work in. EKG and Phlebotomy focus on specific technical skills, while Clinical Medical Assistant and Pharmacy Technician are broader roles. Your AlliedRx instructors can help guide your decision.
What if I need to reschedule my exam? +
You can reschedule your exam through your NHA account. Rescheduling policies and potential fees depend on how close to your exam date you make the change. Check the NHA website for specific rescheduling policies.
What happens if I don't pass my NHA exam? +
You can retake the exam after a waiting period (typically 30 days). You'll need to pay the exam fee again. Use your score report to identify areas for improvement and work with your AlliedRx instructors to strengthen those areas before retesting.
How long is NHA certification valid? +
NHA certifications are valid for two years. To maintain your certification, you must complete continuing education requirements and pay renewal fees. Specific requirements vary by certification type.
Can I take multiple NHA certification exams? +
Yes! Many healthcare professionals hold multiple certifications to increase their job opportunities and versatility. You can apply for additional certifications through your NHA account once you've completed the requirements for each.
