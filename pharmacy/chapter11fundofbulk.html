Container Selection Principles:
1. Light Protection: Amber containers block UV light that degrades light-sensitive medications
2. Moisture Control: Desiccants absorb moisture that causes hydrolysis and degradation
3. Material Compatibility: Glass is inert; some plastics can absorb or leach chemicals
4. Temperature Control: Refrigeration required for suspensions and some liquids
