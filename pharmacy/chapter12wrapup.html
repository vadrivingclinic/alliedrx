Step 2

Key Vocabulary Flashcards

Select each card to reveal the definition. Then try to explain each term in your own words—as if you were training a new pharmacy technician.

Aseptic Technique
Procedures used to eliminate the possibility of a drug becoming contaminated with microbes or particles during preparation.
USP <797>
Chapter 797 of the USP National Formulary containing enforceable standards for compounding sterile preparations, including guidelines, procedures, and compliance requirements.
Clean Room
A contained, controlled environment with low levels of environmental pollutants (dust, airborne microbes, etc.) used for preparing sterile medication products.
Biological Safety Cabinet (BSC)
A vertical flow hood designed to protect the operator and environment when compounding hazardous sterile preparations.
Total Parenteral Nutrition (TPN)
Large‑volume intravenous nutrition administered through a central vein, allowing higher concentration of solutions for patients unable to eat.
Critical Site
Any surface or area exposed to first air that is at risk for touch contamination (e.g., vial tops, open ampules, needle hubs, injection ports).
