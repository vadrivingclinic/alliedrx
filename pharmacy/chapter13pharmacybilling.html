Correct Matches:

Clinical Significance:
Accurate interpretation of insurance cards prevents claim rejections and delays. The BIN identifies which PBM to send the claim to, while the PCN ensures proper routing within that system. Always verify all numbers match exactly what's printed on the card—even one digit off will cause rejection.

✓ Claim Form Analysis
Correct Answers:
• Patient: RODRIGUEZ, MARIA (exactly as shown on ID)
• DOB: 05/14/1982
• Member ID: P735829104 (case-sensitive)
• NDC: 00054369011 (11-digit format - remove dashes, add leading zeros)
• Quantity: 30 tablets
• Days Supply: 30 days (1 tablet daily × 30 tablets)
• Prescriber NPI: 1234567890

Critical Points:
1. NDC Conversion: Must convert 000543-6901-1 to 11 digits: remove dashes = 0005436901, add leading zero = 00054369011
2. Member ID: Must match exactly—including letters and case sensitivity.
3. Name Format: Some systems require LAST, FIRST format exactly as shown on ID.
4. Days Supply Calculation: 30 tablets ÷ 1 tablet/day = 30 days.

Pro Tip: Always verify the NDC conversion and double-check the member ID against the physical card. Most rejections come from data entry errors on these two fields.

💳 Billing & Insurance Insights

  • First-Pass Accuracy: Successful pharmacies achieve 95%+ first-pass claim approval. Each rejection costs an average of 8 minutes of technician time for research and resubmission.
  • Common Rejection Causes: 65% are due to data entry errors (wrong ID, incorrect NDC format). 25% are insurance eligibility issues. The remaining 10% are clinical (PA required, quantity limits).
  • Financial Impact: Clean claims are paid within 14 days. Rejected claims average 30+ days to payment, affecting pharmacy cash flow and requiring more working capital.

📦 Inventory Management Connection

  • NDC Accuracy: The same NDC used in billing must match the NDC in your inventory system. Discrepancies cause billing rejections and inventory variances that require reconciliation.
  • PAR Levels: Understanding billing patterns helps predict medication usage and maintain optimal PAR levels, ensuring medications are available when needed while minimizing tied-up capital.
  • Technician Role: Pharmacy technicians who master both billing accuracy and inventory control become invaluable team members and are well-prepared for supervisory positions.
