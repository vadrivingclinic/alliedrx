Step 2

Key Vocabulary Flashcards

Select each card to reveal the definition. Then try to explain each term in your own words—as if you were training a new pharmacy technician.

Adjudication
The electronic insurance billing process for medication payment where the claim is verified, processed, and approved or rejected by the insurance company.
Formulary
A list of preapproved medications covered under a prescription insurance plan or within an institution; can be open or closed.
Prior Authorization
Insurance-required approval for a restricted, non-formulary, or non-covered medication before a prescription can be filled and reimbursed.
PAR Level
Periodic Automatic Replenishment level; a predetermined inventory quantity that triggers reordering when stock falls below that level.
NDC Number
National Drug Code; a unique 10- or 11-digit identifier for all medications, used for billing, inventory, and database tracking.
Drug Utilization Review
An ongoing review of prescribing, dispensing, and medication use based on predetermined criteria to ensure appropriate and safe drug therapy.
