🛡️ Safety System Insights

  • Layered Defenses: Modern medication safety uses multiple overlapping systems. A single error might be caught by CPOE (ordering), ADS (dispensing), barcode scanning (administration), and smart pumps (administration). This "Swiss cheese model" prevents errors from reaching patients.
  • Reporting Culture: Effective error prevention requires non-punitive reporting. ISMP estimates only 1-10% of errors are reported. Creating a "just culture" where staff feel safe reporting near-misses is essential for system improvement.
  • Human Factors Engineering: The most effective systems work WITH human behavior, not against it. Well-designed barcode scanners, intuitive CPOE interfaces, and clear pump programming reduce cognitive load and prevent slips/lapses.

👨‍⚕️ Pharmacy Technician Role

  • System Monitoring: Technicians are often the first to notice system issues— ADS restocking errors, barcode scanner malfunctions, or pump calibration problems. Proactive monitoring prevents downstream errors.
  • Documentation Accuracy: In Jonathan's scenario, accurate documentation of near-misses helps the safety committee identify patterns. Technicians' detailed logs of "almost errors" are gold mines for system improvement.
  • Patient Education: Technicians can teach patients to be safety partners— "Ask to see the barcode scan" or "Verify your pump settings." This adds another layer of protection.
