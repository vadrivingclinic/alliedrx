⚖️ Ethical Practice Insights

  • Morals vs Ethics Distinction: Morals are your personal compass (what you believe is right). Ethics are the professional road map (rules you must follow). Both guide your practice, but ethics provide the specific legal and professional requirements.
  • Nondiscretionary Duties: These are tasks you MUST perform correctly every time. Errors in counting, documentation, or controlled substance handling aren't just mistakes - they're potential legal violations. Your knowledge of the law protects both patients and your career.
  • Scope of Practice Boundaries: Knowing what you CAN'T do is as important as knowing what you CAN do. Practicing beyond your scope isn't just unsafe - it's illegal. Always verify state-specific regulations and never assume tasks are allowed.
  • Consequence Awareness: Pharmacy violations carry serious weight because they impact public health. HIPAA violations, controlled substance discrepancies, and negligence aren't minor issues - they can end careers and harm patients.
  • Ethical Decision Framework: When in doubt, use the ethical decision-making process: gather facts, identify stakeholders, consider alternatives, apply ethical principles, document everything. This structured approach protects patients and provides legal defensibility.
