Identify HIPAA Violations

Read each scenario and select whether it represents a HIPAA violation or compliant behavior. Understanding these distinctions is crucial for protecting patient privacy.

Scenario 1: A pharmacy technician is processing prescriptions when a family member calls asking about the medications for their elderly parent. The technician recognizes the voice and provides the information over the phone without verifying identity.
A
HIPAA Violation
Cannot disclose PHI without proper verification, even if caller sounds familiar
B
Compliant Behavior
Family members can access information if they sound legitimate
Scenario 2: A pharmacist needs to discuss a patient's complex medication regimen with another pharmacist for consultation. They step into a private office and close the door before having the conversation.
A
HIPAA Violation
Discussing patient information with colleagues always requires written consent
B
Compliant Behavior
Private consultation for treatment purposes is permitted under HIPAA
