The Communication Cycle (Ch4)

  • Sender: Pharmacy technician with message to convey
  • Message: Medication instructions, refill information, etc.
  • Channel: Verbal, written, or nonverbal communication
  • Receiver: Patient, caregiver, or healthcare professional
  • Feedback: Patient questions, clarification requests, understanding verification
Barriers to Effective Communication:
  • Language differences or jargon use
  • Hearing or vision impairments
  • Cultural differences in communication styles
  • Emotional distress or anxiety
  • Environmental noise or distractions
