✓ Storage Requirements Mastered
Correct Storage Categories:

Refrigerated (2-8°C):
• Insulin (unopened), vaccines, latanoprost, amoxicillin suspension
• Why: Proteins denature at room temperature
• Exception: Insulin can be room temp for 28 days after opening

Frozen (Below -20°C):
• Certain biologicals, varicella vaccine
• Why: Extreme cold preserves complex proteins
• Warning: Never refreeze after thawing

Light Protected:
• Nitroglycerin, nifedipine, tetracycline, doxycycline
• Why: Photodegradation causes chemical breakdown
• Solution: Amber bottles, keep in original packaging

Room Temperature:
• Most tablets/capsules (warfarin, metformin, levothyroxine)
• Conditions: Tight container, dry place, away from heat
• Monitoring: Watch for discoloration, unusual odors
