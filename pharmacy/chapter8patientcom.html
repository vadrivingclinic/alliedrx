Scope of Practice Clarifications:
• Technicians CAN: Screen, document, manage inventory, bill, obtain consent
• Technicians CANNOT: Administer vaccines or prescribe (pharmacist-only)
• State Variations: Some states allow techs to administer under protocol (check local laws)

MTM Critical Thinking:
1. Warfarin Monitoring: INR checks every 4-6 weeks are essential for safety
2. Drug Interactions: Atorvastatin can potentiate warfarin effects
3. Patient Factors: Diet (vitamin K), supplements, alcohol affect warfarin
4. Documentation: All interventions must be properly documented

Compliance Standards:
• Immunization documentation must be submitted within 24 hours
• Vaccine storage logs must be maintained daily
• Patient counseling must be offered for all new medications
• Controlled substance documentation requires additional safeguards
