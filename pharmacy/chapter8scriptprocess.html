Key Workflow Principles:
• Sequential Processing: Each step depends on the previous one's completion
• Double Verification: Two pharmacist checks ensure medication safety
• Insurance First: Adjudication happens before preparation to avoid waste
• Patient-Centered: Counseling is the final, crucial safety step

Common Errors to Avoid:
- Preparing medication before insurance approval (financial waste)
- Skipping the second pharmacist verification (safety risk)
- Rushing through data entry (source of most dispensing errors)
