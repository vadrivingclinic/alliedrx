Which federal law requires child-resistant packaging for most oral prescription medications?
✓
Correct Answer
Correct Answer: B. Poison Prevention Packaging Act (PPPA)

The Poison Prevention Packaging Act of 1970 requires child-resistant packaging for most oral prescription drugs, over-the-counter drugs, household chemicals, and other hazardous substances. This law has significantly reduced accidental poisonings in children under 5 years old.
