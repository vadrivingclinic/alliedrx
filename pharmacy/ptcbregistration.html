PTCB Certification Exam Guide

Everything you need to know about registering for, taking, and passing the Pharmacy Technician Certification Exam (PTCE).

📋

Exam Registration

Pharmacy Technician students will be able to register at www.ptcb.org for their national certification exam. The cost to apply for certification and take the Pharmacy Technician Certification Exam (PTCE) is $129.
⏱️

Exam Scheduling

Candidates who successfully apply will be authorized to schedule an exam appointment. You can choose from available dates and testing centers near you through the PTCB scheduling system.
📊

Results Timeline

Official score reports are typically available within three weeks from your exam date. PTCB cannot expedite the processing of exam results or certification information.

What to Expect After the PTCB Examination

1 Exam Completion
Complete your PTCE at your scheduled testing center. You'll receive a preliminary score report immediately after completing the exam.
2 Results Processing
Official score reports are processed and typically available within three weeks from your exam date. PTCB cannot expedite this process.
3 Score Report Availability
Once your exam results are made official, your score report will be available within your PTCB Account.
4 Certification Activation
If you've passed, your certification will become active immediately. Your certificate will be available to download through your PTCB Account.
5 Verification Access
Your certification status will be verifiable through PTCB's Certification Verification page, allowing employers to confirm your credentials.
✅ Passing the Exam

Once you pass the PTCE, you become a Certified Pharmacy Technician (CPhT). This nationally recognized certification opens doors to better job opportunities, higher pay, and career advancement in pharmacy settings across the United States.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I'm eligible to take the PTCE? +
Eligibility requirements include completing a PTCB-recognized education/training program or having equivalent work experience. Visit ptcb.org for detailed eligibility criteria.
What if I need to reschedule my exam? +
You can reschedule your exam through the Pearson VUE website up to 24 hours before your scheduled appointment. Fees may apply for late rescheduling.
What happens if I don't pass the exam? +
You can retake the exam after a 60-day waiting period. You'll need to pay the exam fee again and submit a new application through the PTCB website.
How long is the certification valid? +
PTCB certification is valid for two years. To maintain your certification, you must complete 20 hours of continuing education (including at least 1 hour in pharmacy law and 1 hour in patient safety) and pay the renewal fee.
