Key Vocabulary Flashcards

Select each card to reveal the definition. Then try to explain each term in your own words—as if you were describing it to a new technician.

Medication Reconciliation
The process of preparing a complete and accurate listing of a patient's medications and related medical information.
Managed Care
An organized health care delivery system designed to improve quality and accessibility while containing costs.
Pharmacy Informatics
Practice that includes the use of information technology to ensure optimal medication use.
Telepharmacy
Provision of pharmaceutical care to patients at a distance through telecommunications and information technologies.
Interdisciplinary Team
A health care team from different disciplines working together to provide care for a patient's total needs.
Pharmacy Benefit Management (PBM)
Development and management of cost-efficient prescription drug benefits for large patient populations.
