💊 Pharmacy Technician Insights

  • Blood Thinner Safety: Warfarin (Coumadin) requires regular INR monitoring. Pharmacy technicians must ensure patients understand the importance of consistent dosing and dietary restrictions (Vitamin K foods).
  • Osteoporosis Medication Timing: Bisphosphonates like Alendronate (Fosamax) must be taken with a full glass of water first thing in the morning, at least 30 minutes before food or other medications, and the patient must remain upright.
  • Brand-Generic Proficiency: Accurate brand-generic conversion is essential for preventing medication errors. Technicians should double-check conversions when processing prescriptions, especially with sound-alike drugs.

⚠️ Important Safety Notes

  • Anticoagulant Reversal Agents: Know that Vitamin K reverses warfarin, protamine reverses heparin, and Andexanet alfa reverses factor Xa inhibitors like rivaroxaban.
  • Bisphosphonate Side Effects: Educate patients about possible esophageal irritation, osteonecrosis of the jaw (rare), and atypical femur fractures with long-term use.
  • Storage Requirements: Some osteoporosis medications like Teriparatide (Forteo) require refrigeration. Technicians must verify proper storage and handling.
