Financial & Inventory Operations

Insurance Billing • Inventory Control • Regulatory Compliance • Pharmacy Economics

Week 10 covers pharmacy billing systems and inventory management, the financial backbone of pharmacy operations. You'll learn insurance processing, formulary management, inventory control, and compliance requirements that ensure pharmacy profitability and regulatory adherence.

Effective billing and inventory management directly impact pharmacy viability and patient care. Pharmacy technicians play crucial roles in claims processing, inventory optimization, and ensuring medication availability while controlling costs.

🏥 Insurance Program Types
Medicare
Federal program for age 65+, disabilities, ESRD; Part D covers prescriptions
Medicaid
State/federal program for low-income individuals and families
HMO
Health Maintenance Organization with in-network restrictions and gatekeeper PCP
PPO
Preferred Provider Organization with provider choice options and network discounts
🏛️ Medicare Parts Coverage
Part A: Hospital Insurance
Inpatient hospital care, skilled nursing, hospice, home health
Part B: Medical Insurance
Physician services, outpatient care, preventive services
Part C: Medicare Advantage
Private plan alternative combining Parts A, B, and often D
Part D: Prescription Drug
Standalone prescription drug coverage, formularies vary by plan
📋 Formulary Types Comparison
Open Formulary
Unrestricted drug choices; all prescribed medications typically covered
Closed Formulary
Restricted to pre-approved list; non-formulary drugs require prior auth

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

  • Navigate insurance adjudication processes for Medicare, Medicaid, and private insurance plans.
  • Differentiate between open and closed formularies and their impact on medication coverage.
  • Process third-party claims accurately and resolve common rejection reasons efficiently.
  • Implement PAR level inventory systems to optimize stock while minimizing waste and costs.
  • Apply proper storage requirements and handle recalled/expired medications according to regulations.
  • Complete patient profiles accurately to support billing, drug utilization evaluation, and patient safety.
Billing Efficiency: Point of Sale (POS) systems allow real-time inventory tracking and automatic reordering. When integrated with billing systems, they reduce errors by 75% and improve inventory turnover by optimizing just-in-time ordering practices.
Generic vs Brand: Generic substitution saves patients and insurance companies 80-85% compared to brand name drugs. Technicians must understand DAW codes (0-9) to communicate prescribing preferences and ensure proper billing and patient counseling.
Career Advancement: To advance like Brian, technicians should master inventory optimization, complex insurance billing, and regulatory compliance. Additional certifications in billing systems, inventory management software, and specialized training in government programs enhance promotion potential.
📚 Key Billing & Inventory Terms
Adjudication
Electronic insurance billing for medication payment processing
National Drug Code (NDC)
10-digit unique identifier for all drugs in the United States
Periodic Automatic Replenishment (PAR)
System maintaining stock levels at predetermined unit amounts
Drug Utilization Evaluation (DUE)
Ongoing review of prescribing, dispensing, and medication use patterns
Prior Authorization
Insurance-required approval for nonformulary or restricted medications
Point of Sale (POS)
System tracking inventory as it is sold or used
Average Wholesale Price (AWP)
Average price at which drugs are sold; used for reimbursement calculations
Prime Vendor
Large distributor (McKesson, Cardinal Health) providing bulk medications at contract prices
Just-in-Time Ordering
System ordering products just before needed to minimize inventory costs
