Business Operations

Financial & Operational Pharmacy Excellence

Chapter 13 (Pharmacy Billing & Inventory Management) bridges clinical knowledge with business operations. These skills ensure medication access for patients while maintaining pharmacy financial health and regulatory compliance.

Why Billing & Inventory Matter:

  • Patient Access: Proper billing ensures patients can afford necessary medications
  • Pharmacy Viability: Effective inventory management controls costs and prevents waste
  • Regulatory Compliance: Accurate billing and inventory tracking meet legal requirements
  • Quality Care: Proper stock ensures medication availability when patients need it
Brian's Career Advancement Strategy:
  • Master Billing Systems: Become the go-to expert for complex insurance claims
  • Optimize Inventory: Reduce waste and improve turnover rates
  • Lead DUE Processes: Take initiative in drug utilization evaluation
  • Train Others: Share knowledge with newer technicians
  • Document Achievements: Keep records of cost-saving improvements
Common Claim Rejection Reasons:
  • Incorrect patient ID or group number
  • Medication not on formulary (requires prior auth)
  • Refill too soon (early refill restriction)
  • Dose exceeds plan limits (quantity limit exceeded)
  • Provider not in network (out-of-network claim)
  • Incorrect NDC or date of service

Inventory Management Best Practices:

  • PAR Level Optimization: Balance availability with carrying costs
  • FIFO Rotation: First-in, first-out to minimize expiration losses
  • Temperature Monitoring: Continuous logging for refrigerated items
  • Controlled Substance Security: Double-locked storage, meticulous documentation
  • Regular Audits: Physical counts to verify system accuracy
Government Program Key Differences:
  • Medicare: Federal program for seniors/disabled (Part D for prescriptions)
  • Medicaid: State-federal partnership for low-income individuals
  • Medigap: Private supplemental insurance for Medicare gaps
  • TRICARE: Military health system (formerly CHAMPUS)
  • CHAMPVA: Veterans Affairs program for disabled veterans' families
  • Workers' Comp: Employer-funded injury coverage

Pharmacy Financial Systems Mastery:

  • Third-Party Billing: Electronic claims submission and adjudication
  • Point of Sale (POS): Real-time inventory and sales tracking
  • Prior Authorization: Navigating restricted medication approvals
  • Co-pay Assistance: Manufacturer coupons and discount programs
  • 340B Program: Discounted pricing for covered entities
⚠️ Inventory Management Pitfalls:
  • Overstocking leading to expiration losses
  • Understocking causing patient care delays
  • Poor temperature control compromising medication integrity
  • Inadequate controlled substance documentation risking regulatory violations
  • Failure to process recalls promptly endangering patient safety
