Brand Name Generic Name
Valtrex Acyclovir

Key Information:

Valtrex (Acyclovir):
  • Used for herpes simplex virus (HSV) infections
  • Also treats varicella-zoster virus (shingles)
  • Available in oral and IV formulations
  • Common side effect: nausea
Virazole (Ribavirin):
  • Used for respiratory syncytial virus (RSV)
  • Also treats hepatitis C (in combination)
  • Administered via aerosol or orally
  • Important: Teratogenic - avoid in pregnancy

💊 Pharmacy Technician Insights

  • Acyclovir (Valtrex) Administration: This antiviral requires adequate hydration during IV administration to prevent crystalluria. Pharmacy technicians should ensure proper reconstitution and storage conditions.
  • Ribavirin (Virazole) Safety: Extremely teratogenic - must have strict pregnancy prevention protocols. Technicians should verify pregnancy tests for female patients and ensure proper counseling about contraception requirements.
  • Brand-Generic Verification: With antiviral medications, accurate brand-generic conversion is critical for insurance billing and preventing medication errors, especially with sound-alike drugs.

⚠️ Important Safety Notes

  • Renal Function Monitoring: Acyclovir requires dose adjustment for patients with renal impairment. Technicians should flag prescriptions for patients with known renal issues for pharmacist review.
  • Respiratory Administration: Ribavirin aerosol must be administered using specialized equipment. Technicians should verify the correct delivery system is available and provide appropriate patient education materials.
  • Storage Requirements: Some antiviral formulations require specific storage conditions. Technicians must verify proper storage and check expiration dates regularly due to stability concerns.
