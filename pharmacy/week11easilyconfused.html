💊 Pharmacy Technician Safety Standards

  • Verification Protocols: Always verify medication names using at least two identifiers. When receiving verbal orders, use the "read-back" technique: repeat the medication name and spelling to the prescriber.
  • Shelf Organization: Implement physical separation of look-alike medications. Use shelf labels with Tall Man lettering and warning stickers for high-risk pairs.
  • Computer System Safety: Configure pharmacy software to flag look-alike/sound-alike medications. Use alerts when selecting medications with similar names or when dispensing high-risk pairs.

⚠️ ISMP Recommendations

  • Standardize Tall Man Lettering: Use ISMP-recommended Tall Man lettering consistently across all pharmacy documentation, labels, and computer systems.
  • Error Reporting: Report all near-misses and actual errors involving look-alike/sound-alike medications to your pharmacy's quality improvement program and to the ISMP.
  • Continuous Education: Regularly review look-alike/sound-alike lists and participate in medication safety training. Share lessons learned with pharmacy team members.
