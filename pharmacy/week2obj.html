Legal & Ethical Foundations

Federal Laws • Regulatory Agencies • Professional Ethics

Week 2 covers the critical legal and ethical frameworks that govern pharmacy practice. You'll learn about federal drug laws, regulatory agencies, controlled substance handling, and the ethical responsibilities of pharmacy technicians.

⚖️ Legal Responsibility

Pharmacy technicians perform nondiscretionary tasks but must still understand pharmacy law thoroughly. Ignorance of the law is not a defense, and technicians can face legal consequences for violations, even if unintentional.

🎯 By the end of this week, you will be able to:

  • Navigate federal drug laws and understand their chronological development.
  • Apply HIPAA regulations to protect patient privacy in pharmacy settings.
  • Handle controlled substances with proper documentation and security protocols.
  • Identify and report adverse drug reactions to the FDA using proper channels.
  • Differentiate between Schedule I-V controlled substances and their corresponding requirements.
  • Verify DEA numbers and identify potentially fraudulent prescriptions.
  • Apply OSHA safety guidelines to maintain a secure pharmacy environment.
  • Distinguish between personal morals and professional ethics in pharmacy practice.
📚 Key Terms & Definitions
HIPAA
Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act - protects patient privacy and health information.
Controlled Substance
Drugs regulated by the DEA, categorized into Schedules I-V based on abuse potential.
Boxed Warning
Strongest FDA warning indicating significant risk of dangerous side effects (Black Box Warning).
NDC
National Drug Code - 10-digit identifier for drug manufacturer, product, and package size.
PHI
Protected Health Information - patient data protected under HIPAA regulations.
SDS
Safety Data Sheet - OSHA-required document with chemical safety information.
Legend Drug
Prescription medication requiring "Federal law prohibits dispensing without a prescription" label.
Drug Diversion
Illegal channeling of prescription drugs from legitimate distribution to illegal use.
💡 HIPAA Tip: Never discuss patient information within earshot of others. When verifying prescriptions over the phone, confirm patient identity with at least two identifiers (name + date of birth OR name + phone number).
⚠️ Controlled Substance Security: Schedule II medications require separate, locked storage with limited access. Maintain accurate inventory records and report any discrepancies immediately to the pharmacist-in-charge.
🔍 DEA Number Verification: Valid DEA numbers follow a specific formula: First letter = registrant type (A, B, or M), second letter = first letter of last name. Use the DEA Number Verification Formula to check legitimacy: Add digits in positions 1, 3, 5, then add digits in positions 2, 4, 6 multiplied by 2. Sum should end with the digit in position 7.
