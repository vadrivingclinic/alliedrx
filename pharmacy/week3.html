📜 National Certification Comparison
PTCB (Pharmacy Technician Certification Board)
Most widely recognized; 90 questions; computer-based; requires high school diploma or equivalent.
NHA (National Healthcareer Association)
100 questions; more scenario-based; offered through ICPT (Institute for the Certification of Pharmacy Technicians).
Maintenance Requirements
Both require 20 hours of continuing education every 2 years, including 1 hour in pharmacy law.
Designation
Certified technicians can use "CPhT" after their name.
📚 Key Terms & Definitions
Nondiscretionary Duties
Tasks that don't require professional judgment: inventory, filling, repackaging, billing.
CPhT
Certified Pharmacy Technician - designation after passing national certification exam.
ASHP Model Curriculum
Guidelines for pharmacy technician education and training program standards.
Closed-Door Pharmacy
Serves institutions like long-term care facilities; not open to the public.
Professionalism
Conforming to accepted work ethics and conduct standards in the profession.
Total Parenteral Nutrition (TPN)
Intravenous nutrition for patients unable to eat; prepared in sterile compounding.
PTAC
Pharmacy Technician Accreditation Commission - accredits technician education programs.
Continuing Education (CE)
Required education for certification maintenance and professional development.
💡 Professional Tip: Join at least one professional association (AAPT, NPTA, or SEPHT) during your training. Membership provides access to continuing education, job boards, and networking events that can accelerate your career.
🏆 Certification Benefits: Certified technicians earn 10-20% higher wages on average, have better job security, and qualify for more advanced positions. Many states now require certification for pharmacy technician registration.
🤝 Networking Strategy: Create a professional LinkedIn profile highlighting your pharmacy technician training and certifications. Connect with instructors, classmates, and professionals you meet during externships. Attend local pharmacy association meetings to build your professional network.
