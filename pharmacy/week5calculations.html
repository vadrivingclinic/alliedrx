📚 Course Overview

To begin the Basic Pharmacy Calculations Course, you need to access your student portal. The course can be found in the portal, where you will find all the necessary materials and resources.

The Basic Pharmacy Calculations Course is designed to help you develop essential skills in pharmaceutical calculations. This course is crucial for anyone pursuing a career in pharmacy, as it covers the fundamental calculations involved in dispensing medications and ensuring proper dosage.

Throughout this part of the program, you will learn various concepts such as unit conversions, dilutions, calculations involving percentages, and dosages based on patient weight. These skills are vital for accurately preparing and administering medications in a pharmacy setting.

As you progress through the course, you will have access to interactive exercises, quizzes, and practice problems to reinforce your understanding of the material.

Remember: Allocate dedicated time each week to study and complete the course requirements. It is recommended to stay organized and create a study plan to ensure you cover all the necessary topics within the given timeframe.
