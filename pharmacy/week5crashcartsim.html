💊 Medication Management Insights

  • Expiration Date Vigilance: Crash cart medications must be checked monthly. Expired medications can lose potency or become harmful, compromising emergency care.
  • Pediatric Specificity: Pediatric crash carts contain weight-based dosing and child-appropriate equipment. Never substitute adult medications for pediatric use.
  • Restocking Protocols: After each use, crash carts must be completely restocked and checked within 24 hours to ensure readiness for the next emergency.

🏥 Pharmacy Technician Role

  • Inventory Management: Technicians are responsible for regular checks, documentation, and timely replacement of crash cart medications.
  • Emergency Preparedness: Understanding which medications are stocked and their locations is critical during code blue situations.
  • Quality Assurance: Proper documentation of checks ensures regulatory compliance and patient safety during emergencies.
