📚 Study Tips for Drug Names

  • Look for patterns: Many generic names share common suffixes (-azole, -oxacin, -cillin)
  • Create mnemonics: Associate brand names with visual or word associations
  • Practice daily: Review 5-10 drug pairs each day for better retention
  • Focus on look-alike/sound-alike: Pay special attention to drugs with similar names

💊 Pharmacy Technician Clinical Pearls

  • Always double-check: Brand-generic confusion is a leading cause of medication errors
  • Know your suffixes: -azole (antifungals), -oxacin (fluoroquinolones), -cillin (penicillins)
  • Patient counseling: Explain that generic medications have the same active ingredient but may look different
  • Look-alike sound-alike: Lamictal (lamotrigine) vs. Lamisil (terbinafine) - very different drugs!
