💉 Phlebotomy Tip
The median cubital vein is typically the first choice for venipuncture because it's usually large, well-anchored, and less likely to roll. It also tends to be less painful for patients.

✓ Quiz Analysis Complete
Answers:
1. C. Median cubital vein - First choice for routine venipuncture
2. B. Front of the elbow - "Ante" means before/front, "cubital" refers to elbow
3. C. Basilic vein - Located near nerves, requires extra caution

Clinical Importance: Proper vein selection minimizes patient discomfort and reduces the risk of complications like nerve damage or hematoma formation.

💡 Clinical Practice Insights

  • Anatomical Precision: Using correct terms like "antecubital" instead of "inner elbow" ensures clear communication with healthcare team members and accurate documentation.
  • Vein Selection Strategy: Always assess multiple factors - vein size, depth, anchoring, and patient comfort. The median cubital vein is preferred when available, but be prepared with alternatives.
  • Patient Safety: Understanding anatomy helps avoid nerves (near basilic vein) and arteries. Proper technique prevents complications and ensures quality specimens.
