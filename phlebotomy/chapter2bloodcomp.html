Blood Component Laboratory

Use the centrifuge simulation to separate blood components, then explore each component's function and characteristics. Understand the differences between plasma, serum, and formed elements.

🔬 Lab Insight
Serum vs Plasma: Serum is plasma without clotting factors. When you see a red-top tube (no additive), it will clot naturally, and after centrifugation, you get serum. Lavender-top tubes (with EDTA) prevent clotting, so after centrifugation, you get plasma.

Homeostasis in Action

⚖️ Homeostasis Example
When you exercise:
1. Muscles need more oxygen → Heart rate increases
2. More CO₂ produced → Breathing rate increases
3. Body temperature rises → Sweating begins
4. After exercise → Systems return to normal

As a phlebotomist, understanding homeostasis explains why patient activity before blood draw can affect results!

💉 Phlebotomy Application Insights

  • Tube Selection: Understanding blood components helps you choose correct tubes. EDTA tubes (lavender) preserve cells for CBC, while serum tubes (red) allow clotting for chemistry tests.
  • Specimen Integrity: Knowing circulation helps explain why tourniquet time matters - prolonged use can alter test results by causing hemoconcentration.
  • Patient Education: You can explain why different tests require different tubes, helping reduce patient anxiety and improving compliance.
