🩺 Did You Know?
Korotkoff sounds are the tapping sounds you hear when taking blood pressure. Phase I (first sound) = systolic pressure. Phase V (disappearance) = diastolic pressure. Missing these can lead to inaccurate readings!

🩸 Clinical Fact
Universal Donor & Recipient: Type O-negative is the universal donor (can give to anyone). Type AB-positive is the universal recipient (can receive from anyone). However, crossmatching is still essential for safety!

Key Principles:
• Hemostasis: The body's natural process to stop bleeding
• Blood Typing: Based on ABO and Rh antigen systems
• Compatibility: Recipient antibodies must not attack donor antigens

Clinical Safety: Always verify blood type twice before transfusion. Acute hemolytic reactions can be fatal within minutes if incompatible blood is given.

  • Blood Bank Basics: Understanding typing helps explain why certain tests require special collection tubes and handling.
