🔬 Diagnostic Puzzle Insights

  • Test-Specific Tubes: aPTT requires light blue sodium citrate tubes with exact 9:1 blood:anticoagulant ratio. Incorrect tubes (like EDTA or heparin) invalidate coagulation results due to different anticoagulant mechanisms.
  • PPE Hierarchy: Contact Precautions require gloves and gown for C. diff, plus dedicated equipment. Standard Precautions (gloves only) are insufficient for spore-forming organisms that contaminate environments.
  • Equipment Failure Protocol: Broken tubes with compromised barriers must be treated as potentially contaminated. Redraw with new equipment and proper documentation prevents lab errors and ensures patient safety.

🏥 Pharmacy Technician Connection

  • Medication Monitoring: Understanding test requirements (like aPTT for heparin) helps technicians anticipate lab needs and ensure proper specimen collection for therapeutic drug monitoring.
  • Infection Control: Technicians handling medications for isolated patients must understand PPE requirements to prevent cross-contamination during preparation and delivery.
  • Quality Assurance: Recognizing and reporting compromised specimens (like broken tubes) is part of the technician's role in maintaining medication safety and efficacy.
