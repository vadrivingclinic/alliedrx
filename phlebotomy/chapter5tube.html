🩸 Phlebotomy Technique Insights

  • Order of Draw is Critical: The CLSI-established order prevents additive carryover that can compromise test results. Blood cultures → Light blue → Red/Gold → Green → Lavender → Gray is the standard sequence.
  • Equipment Selection Matters: 21G needles are standard for most adults, while butterflies (23G) are better for fragile veins. Microtainers and shallow lancets are essential for pediatric draws to minimize blood loss.
  • Proper Tray Setup Saves Time: A well-stocked phlebotomy tray with all essentials (tourniquet, alcohol, gauze, bandages, sharps container) prevents delays during rounds and ensures patient safety.

🏥 Pharmacy Technician Connection

  • Medication Administration: Understanding phlebotomy helps technicians anticipate lab needs for medication monitoring (vancomycin troughs, heparin PT/PTT, chemotherapy CBCs).
  • Specimen Handling: Technicians often receive lab specimens; knowing proper tube types helps verify correct collection and prevent lab rejections.
  • Patient Education: Technicians can explain why multiple tubes are needed and reassure patients about the safety of modern collection techniques.
