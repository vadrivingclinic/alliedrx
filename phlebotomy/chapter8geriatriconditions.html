Age: 82 years
Diagnoses: Moderate dementia, macular degeneration (severe vision loss), osteoarthritis (both hands), osteoporosis, atrial fibrillation on warfarin
Admission: Suspected osteomyelitis (bone infection) in right foot
Setting: Hospital inpatient, room 402
Time: 2:00 PM - Afternoon medication administration time
Complex geriatric patient with multiple interacting conditions

The phlebotomist entered the room and immediately asked the 85-year-old patient to extend her arm. Without explaining the procedure, she applied the tourniquet very tightly and left it on for 3 minutes while searching for a vein. Finding a small rolling vein, she vigorously slapped the area to make it more visible. After insertion, blood flow was slow, so she asked the patient to pump her fist repeatedly. The light blue top tube was only half filled before she removed it. Finally, she applied pressure for only 15 seconds before applying the bandage.

Errors Identified:
