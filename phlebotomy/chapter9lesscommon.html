⚕️ Clinical Management Insights

  • Syncope Protocol: For fainting patients, priority is airway, breathing, circulation. Lower head, ensure safety, then assess vitals. Never leave a syncopal patient unattended.
  • Hematoma Prevention: Apply firm pressure for 3-5 minutes post-draw. For patients on anticoagulants, apply pressure for 5-10 minutes and check site before releasing.
  • Nerve Injury Recognition: Sharp, shooting pain or electric shock sensation indicates possible nerve contact. Immediately withdraw needle and document the incident.

📋 Documentation Requirements

  • Incident Reporting: All complications require documentation including time, symptoms, interventions, and patient response. Report significant events to supervisor immediately.
  • Patient Education: After any complication, educate patient on what to expect (bruising, soreness) and when to seek medical attention (severe pain, swelling).
  • Quality Improvement: Use complication data to identify trends and improve technique. Higher rates of hematomas may indicate need for pressure application training.
