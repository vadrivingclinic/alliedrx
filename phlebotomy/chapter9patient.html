🩸 Phlebotomy Technician Insights

  • Patient Assessment First: Always assess the patient and collection site before equipment preparation. This prevents wasted supplies and patient discomfort.
  • Document Everything: Unsuccessful attempts, patient refusals, and site contraindications require documentation just like successful collections.
  • Short Draw Protocol: Never pour blood between tubes. A short draw in additive tubes requires recollection to maintain proper blood-to-additive ratios.

⚠️ Critical Contraindications

  • Mastectomy Side: Never draw from the same side as a mastectomy due to risk of lymphedema. Always use the opposite arm or consult the patient's chart.
  • IV Lines: Never draw above an IV line. Draw below it or from the opposite arm to prevent specimen contamination with IV fluids.
  • Edematous Areas: Avoid swollen limbs as they yield diluted specimens with altered electrolyte concentrations.
