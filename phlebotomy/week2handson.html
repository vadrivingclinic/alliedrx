Week 2 Objectives

Venipuncture Skill Development

🎯 Primary Focus: Evacuated Tube System

This week you will master the evacuated tube system, the most common venipuncture method used in healthcare settings.

  • ✓ Proper needle gauge selection for different patient types
  • ✓ Correct tourniquet application and vein selection
  • ✓ Mastering the 15-30 degree needle insertion angle
  • ✓ Proper tube order of draw and handling
  • ✓ Safe needle disposal and post-procedure care

Safety

Critical Safety Protocols

Week 2 Safety Focus

These safety protocols are emphasized during venipuncture practice:

  • Needle Safety: Never recap used needles
  • Sharps Disposal: Immediate disposal after use
  • Patient Positioning: Prevent syncope (fainting)
  • Specimen Handling: Prevent hemolysis and contamination
  • PPE Compliance: Gloves and eye protection at all times

Assessment

Week 2 Competency Checklist

By the end of Week 2, you should be able to demonstrate:

  • Proper vein selection and assessment
  • Correct tourniquet application and removal
  • Accurate needle insertion at appropriate angle
  • Proper tube filling and order of draw
  • Safe needle disposal and cleanup
  • Accurate specimen labeling and handling
