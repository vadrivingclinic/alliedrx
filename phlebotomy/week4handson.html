Week 4 Objectives

Mastering Difficult Venipuncture

🎯 Primary Focus: Challenging Patient Scenarios

This week focuses on developing advanced skills for patients with difficult venous access and complex medical conditions that make standard venipuncture challenging.

  • ✓ Advanced vein location and assessment techniques
  • ✓ Special approaches for obese patients and edema
  • ✓ Techniques for fragile, rolling, and collapsing veins
  • ✓ Alternative site selection and positioning strategies
  • ✓ When to use syringe method vs. evacuated tube system

Clinical Scenarios

Difficult Patient Scenarios

Advanced Problem Solving

Develop strategies for these challenging patient situations:

  • Obese Patients: Deep vein location and extended needle use
  • Elderly Patients: Fragile veins and skin considerations
  • IV Drug Users: Scarred veins and alternative sites
  • Cancer Patients: Chemotherapy effects and limited access
  • Burn Patients: Limited sites and specialized techniques

Equipment Selection & Decision Making

Advanced Clinical Skills

Develop critical thinking skills for equipment selection:

  • Needle Gauge Selection: Matching gauge to patient and vein
  • Syringe vs. Vacutainer: When to use each method
  • Butterfly Applications: Specific indications and techniques
  • Tourniquet Alternatives: When standard tourniquets fail
  • When to Stop: Recognizing limits and seeking assistance

Assessment
