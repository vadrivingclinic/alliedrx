Patient Care

Special Population Considerations

Adapting Techniques

Learn to adapt your approach for different patient populations:

  • Pediatric Patients: Age-appropriate explanations, distraction techniques
  • Geriatric Patients: Fragile skin considerations, positioning assistance
  • Needle-Phobic Patients: Anxiety reduction techniques
  • Patients with Disabilities: Adaptive positioning and communication

Troubleshooting

Capillary Collection Complications

Learn to recognize and manage common capillary collection issues:

Insufficient Sample
Techniques to improve blood flow and adequate collection
Hemolysis Prevention
Proper technique to avoid red blood cell destruction
Clotting Issues
Managing rapid clotting in small volume samples
Site Bleeding
Proper pressure application and follow-up care
