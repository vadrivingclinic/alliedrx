Venipuncture Procedure Steps

1
Patient Identification
Greet patient, verify identity using 2 identifiers, explain procedure
2
Hand Hygiene & PPE
Wash hands, don gloves and appropriate PPE
3
Site Selection & Preparation
Select appropriate vein, apply tourniquet, clean site with alcohol
4
Venipuncture
Anchor vein, insert needle at 15-30° angle, establish blood flow
5
Tube Filling & Mixing
Fill tubes in correct order, gently invert additive tubes 8-10 times
6
Needle Removal & Site Care
Release tourniquet, remove needle, apply pressure, bandage
7
Specimen Labeling & Disposal
Label at bedside, activate safety device, dispose in sharps container

Critical Procedure Differences:
  • Blood Culture vs Routine: Double skin prep (alcohol → chlorhexidine), larger volume (10-20mL)
  • Arterial vs Venous: Radial/brachial arteries, heparinized syringe, ice transport
  • Butterfly vs ETS: 21-23g wings, flexible tubing, Luer adapter, 10-15° angle
  • Microcapillary vs Venipuncture: Finger/heel stick, lancets, EDTA-coated tubes
  • Therapeutic Collection: Polycythemia vera, hemochromatosis, large volume removal
Patient Safety Protocols:
  • 2-Patient Identifiers: Always verify name & date of birth BEFORE collection
  • Time-Critical Collections: Glucose tolerance, peak/trough drug levels, STAT orders
  • Special Handling: Light protection (bilirubin), ice (ABGs), warmth (cold agglutinins)
  • Infection Control: Blood cultures require maximal barrier precautions
  • Documentation: Time, site, technique, patient tolerance, complications
When to Use Each Technique:
  • Evacuated Tube System: Routine collections, most adult patients
  • Butterfly Needle: Fragile veins, hand veins, pediatric patients
  • Syringe: Difficult veins, small volume needs, no vacuum pressure
  • Microcapillary: Pediatric patients, point-of-care testing, small volumes
  • Arterial Puncture: ABG analysis, respiratory status monitoring

Clinical Scenarios to Anticipate:

  • Collecting blood cultures on a febrile patient with suspected septicemia
  • Performing timed collections for a 3-hour glucose tolerance test
  • Using butterfly technique on an elderly patient with fragile, rolling veins
  • Collecting therapeutic phlebotomy for polycythemia vera patient
  • Preparing perfect blood smears for malaria parasite examination
