Step 3

Key Vocabulary Flashcards

Select each card to reveal the definition. These terms are essential for understanding venipuncture and special procedures.

Basal State
The resting metabolic state of the body early in the morning after fasting for 12 hours. Important for standardization of lab results.
Analyte
A substance or chemical constituent that is undergoing analysis (e.g., glucose, cholesterol, sodium).
Diurnal Variation
Normal fluctuations in body functions and analyte levels that occur during a 24-hour period.
Luer Adapter
A device that allows a butterfly needle to be used with an evacuated tube holder.
Aerobic vs. Anaerobic
Aerobic: Requires oxygen for growth. Anaerobic: Grows without oxygen. Important for blood culture collection.
Allen Test
A test performed before arterial puncture to ensure adequate collateral circulation in the hand.

Special Collection Procedures

Important Special Tests

Review these key special procedures that skilled phlebotomists may perform:

Blood Cultures
Critical for detecting septicemia; requires strict aseptic technique and specific collection timing.
Glucose Tolerance Test (GTT)
Measures body's response to glucose; requires timed collections at fasting, 1hr, 2hr, and sometimes 3hr intervals.
Arterial Blood Gases (ABGs)
Measures oxygen and carbon dioxide levels; requires special training and Allen test verification.
Bleeding Time Test
Assesses platelet function; requires standardized incision and timing measurement.
Therapeutic Phlebotomy
Removal of blood for treatment of conditions like polycythemia vera or hemochromatosis.

Scenario Reflection

You're asked to collect blood cultures from a patient with suspected septicemia. The order specifies "aerobic and anaerobic bottles" with specific timing requirements.

Step 4

Venipuncture Procedure Checklist

Review these critical steps for successful venipuncture. Check each item when you can perform it confidently.

1
Patient Identification: Verify patient using two identifiers (name & DOB), check requisition form.
2
Equipment Preparation: Gather appropriate tubes, needle, tourniquet, gauze, bandage, labels.
3
Patient Preparation: Explain procedure, position patient, apply tourniquet 3-4 inches above site.
4
Site Selection & Prep: Select appropriate vein, clean site with alcohol using circular motion, allow to dry.
5
Venipuncture: Anchor vein, insert needle bevel up at 15-30 degree angle, collect tubes in correct order.
6
Post-Procedure: Release tourniquet, remove needle, apply pressure, label tubes, dispose of sharps properly.

Remember: Always follow institutional protocols and prioritize patient safety and comfort.
