Foundations & Safety Protocols

Study Skills • Emergency Response • Professionalism

Week 1 establishes the professional and safety groundwork. You'll explore how to learn effectively in a competency-based program and how to keep patients (and yourself) safe during emergencies.

These chapters provide the toolkit for your success as a student and a future professional: mastering how to learn and how to respond when seconds count.

🧠 Chapter 1 Case Study: Shawna Long
Situation: Newly admitted MA student, anxious about success, works part-time.
Challenge: Balancing work/school, overcoming past academic struggles.
Key Tools: Learning styles analysis, time management, adaptive coping mechanisms.
🚑 Chapter 12 Case Study: Cheryl Skurka
Situation: Working for 6 months, concerned about emergency preparedness.
Challenge: Needs to learn telephone screening for emergencies and disaster response.
Key Tools: Emergency protocols, community resources, drill participation.

Key Skills Focus:

  • CPR & First Aid: Provider-level response for cardiac arrest, bleeding, and shock.
  • Fire Safety: RACE acronym and PASS technique for extinguishers.
  • Triage: Telephone screening to identify life-threatening conditions.
  • Self-Management: Time management and stress reduction strategies.
Emergency Supplies: Familiarize yourself with the Crash Cart. Know the location of the AED, oxygen tank, and emergency medications (epinephrine, nitroglycerin) in your facility.
Study Tip: Use Mnemonics to memorize lists (e.g., PASS for fire extinguishers, CAB for CPR). Active Reflection helps connect new knowledge to real-world scenarios.
Legal Note: In emergencies, always act within your Scope of Practice. Provide care you are trained and authorized to give, and document all actions thoroughly.
