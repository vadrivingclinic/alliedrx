Course Skills

Hands-on Skill Development

SKILL 10 - Venipuncture

Perform blood draws using the Evacuated Tube System (ETS) and Winged Infusion Sets (Butterfly).

SKILL 11 - Capillary Puncture

Perform finger and heel sticks with proper site selection, warming, and collection.

SKILL 12 - CLIA-Waived Testing

Perform Urinalysis (dipstick), rapid HCG, Mono, and other point-of-care tests safely.

Before You Arrive

Lab Readiness Checklist

Before attending your first lab session, ensure you have:

  • Reviewed Order of Draw (colored tubes)
  • Studied Universal Precautions and PPE
  • Completed Week 4 Pre-Lab Quiz
  • Confirmed your session time (daytime or evening)
