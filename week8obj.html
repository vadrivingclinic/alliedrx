Medication Safety & Musculoskeletal Care

Drug Therapy • Orthopedic Procedures • Pain Management

Week 8 combines pharmacology principles with orthopedic specialization, creating essential knowledge for managing musculoskeletal disorders. You'll learn medication safety protocols while developing expertise in orthopedic assessments, therapeutic modalities, and rehabilitation techniques for comprehensive patient care.

These chapters connect through pain management and medication safety. Orthopedic patients often require analgesics, anti-inflammatories, and muscle relaxants. Understanding pharmacology ensures safe medication use while orthopedic knowledge provides context for appropriate drug selection and monitoring.

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

  • Manage controlled substances according to DEA regulations and practice protocols.
  • Accurately transcribe prescriptions and utilize e-prescribing systems for orthopedic medications.
  • Apply appropriate therapeutic modalities (heat/cold) for musculoskeletal injuries.
  • Fit patients with assistive devices and teach proper ambulation techniques.
  • Integrate pharmacology knowledge with orthopedic care for comprehensive pain management.
Orthopedic Pharmacology Tip: NSAIDs (nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs) like ibuprofen and naproxen are commonly prescribed for orthopedic pain but require monitoring for GI bleeding and renal function. Always assess for contraindications and educate patients about taking with food.
Controlled Substance Protocol: Schedule II medications (oxycodone, hydrocodone) for orthopedic pain require written prescriptions with no refills. Document pain assessment scores before and after administration. Monitor for signs of misuse or diversion in patients with chronic pain conditions.
RICE Protocol: For acute musculoskeletal injuries: Rest, Ice, Compression, Elevation. Apply ice for 15-20 minutes every 2-3 hours for first 48 hours. Use barrier between ice and skin to prevent frostbite. Switch to heat therapy after acute phase (48-72 hours).
Crutch Fitting Protocol: Proper crutch height = 1-1.5 inches below axilla with shoulders relaxed. Hand grips at wrist crease when arms hang naturally. Teach three-point gait for non-weight bearing and four-point gait for partial weight bearing. Never lean on axillary bars.
