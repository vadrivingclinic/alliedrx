Precision Math & Neurologic Expertise

Medication Calculations • Neurologic Assessment • Mental Health Care

Week 9 combines critical medication calculation skills with specialized neurologic knowledge. You'll learn precise math techniques for safe medication administration while developing expertise in assessing and caring for patients with nervous system and mental health disorders.

These chapters connect through medication precision for neurologic conditions. Accurate dosing is especially critical for neurologic medications like anticonvulsants, while understanding neurologic disorders ensures appropriate medication administration and monitoring for patients with complex conditions.

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

  • Accurately calculate medication doses using multiple mathematical methods and verify calculations.
  • Convert between measurement systems and calculate pediatric doses using weight-based formulas.
  • Assist with neurologic examinations including cranial nerve assessment and coordination testing.
  • Prepare patients for neurologic diagnostic procedures (EEG, lumbar puncture) with proper education.
  • Recognize symptoms of common neurologic and mental health disorders and provide appropriate support.
Medication Calculation Safety: Always use the "Double-Check" rule for medication calculations. Calculate independently, then verify using a different method (ratio-proportion vs. dimensional analysis). For pediatric doses, calculate based on weight in kg and have another healthcare professional verify before administration.
Neurologic Medication Precision: Anticonvulsant medications like phenytoin and valproic acid have narrow therapeutic ranges. Small calculation errors can lead to toxicity or treatment failure. Always verify doses and monitor for side effects specific to neurologic medications.
Seizure Safety Protocol: During a seizure: Time the seizure, protect from injury (move furniture, cushion head), turn patient on side, never restrain or put anything in mouth. Document duration, type of movements, and post-ictal state. Administer emergency medications only as ordered.
Mental Health Sensitivity: When working with mental health patients: Use non-judgmental language, maintain appropriate boundaries, validate feelings without reinforcing delusions. Document objectively using direct quotes when possible. Know emergency protocols for suicidal ideation or aggressive behavior.
